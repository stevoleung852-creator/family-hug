Family Hug Privacy Policy

Effective 25 October 2026

In short: Family Hug has no accounts and no servers. Your family's information is stored only on your iPhone. We don't collect it, see it, sell it or track you. It only leaves your iPhone when you choose to share a backup, turn on an optional feature that uses your own account with another service, or leave an optional tip.

What Family Hug stores, and where

Everything you add to Family Hug stays in the app's private storage on your iPhone. That includes family members, calendar events, lists, meals, recipes, budget entries, bills, pets and their care, school timetables, documents and contacts. Your iPhone includes this in its normal device backups, such as iCloud Backup or an encrypted backup to your computer, under your Apple Account.

The developer of Family Hug has no server, database or account system, and never receives this information.

What we collect

Nothing about your family. Family Hug contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code. The only third-party code is RevenueCat, used solely for optional tips (see below), and it only runs if you open the tip screen. If you join a TestFlight beta, Apple may share crash reports and feedback you choose to send, under Apple's privacy policy.

Permissions on your iPhone

  • Calendars, only if you turn on "Show Apple Calendar Events". Events are read on your iPhone to show alongside your family events. Family Hug doesn't change or upload them.
  • Camera, only when you scan a document. Scans are saved in the app on your iPhone, or added to your message if you scan from the assistant.
  • Photos, only the photos you pick, for example for a recipe, a pet or a message to the assistant.
  • Notifications, only if you set reminders for bills or pet care. Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone.

Optional features that use other services

These are off until you set them up, and use your own account with each service.

  • AI Assistant with Apple Intelligence. On iPhones that support it, the assistant can use Apple Intelligence, which runs entirely on your iPhone. Photos and files you add to a message are read on your iPhone too. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.
  • AI Assistant with Claude or ChatGPT. If you add your own Claude (Anthropic) or ChatGPT (OpenAI) API key, the question you ask, any photo, screenshot or PDF you add to it, and the categories of family information you've allowed in Settings › AI Assistant (which can include Apple Calendar events, if you show them in Family Hug) are sent directly from your iPhone to that provider, using your key, when you ask something. Their own privacy policies apply: Anthropic, OpenAI. Your key is stored in your iPhone's Keychain.
  • Price Check. If you connect your own WhichGrocer account, you sign in on WhichGrocer's own page; Family Hug never sees your password. When you compare prices or search for products, the item names or search words are sent to WhichGrocer. Product photos in results are loaded from the stores' websites. WhichGrocer's own terms and privacy policy apply. Your sign-in is stored in your iPhone's Keychain, and you can disconnect it in Settings › Price Check.
  • Tips. Settings › Support Family Hug offers optional tips, which unlock nothing. Payments are handled by Apple under your Apple Account; we never see your card or payment details. To record a tip, the tip screen uses RevenueCat, which receives the purchase details (which tip, price, date and country), a random anonymous ID created on your iPhone, and basic device information such as the iOS version. It doesn't receive your name, email or family's information. RevenueCat's privacy policy applies. RevenueCat is only contacted if you open the tip screen.
  • Product links. Opening a "View at" link takes you to that store's website in your browser.

Backups you share

Settings › Backup & Export creates a file with your family's information, for example to move it to a new iPhone or share it with a partner. You choose where it goes. The file isn't password-protected, so only share it with people you trust. API keys and sign-ins are never included.

Deleting your information

Settings › Erase All Data removes everything from the app. Deleting Family Hug from your iPhone also deletes its data. Information you sent to an optional service (an AI provider or WhichGrocer) is handled by that service; contact them to delete it.

Children

Families can add details about their children, such as names, birthdays and school timetables. This stays on the family's iPhone like everything else. Family Hug doesn't collect information from anyone.

Changes
